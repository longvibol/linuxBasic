Because the echo perform word quoting it will remove the space 

>> echo hello             hi 
==> hello hi 

we can not print the space in between 

>> echo "hello            hi"
hello            hi

- when it have $ sign it will think for the variable 

Ex: 

>>echo "holly $hit"
holly 


>> echo "today is .... $(date)"
today is .... Mon Oct 27 02:32:30 PM +07 2025

>> echo 'holy $hit'

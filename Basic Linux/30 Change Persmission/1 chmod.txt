chmod = change mode 

- chmod = change permission 

syntax: 

>> chmod mode file


- u = user
- g = group
- o = others
- a = all 

- sing " - " remove permission 
- sing " + " add permission 

>> chmod a=rwx filename


Example:


vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ chmod g-r MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ ls -l | grep MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
-rwx---r--  1 vibol vibol      1410 Nov 19 08:58 MS_LIG Nov 2025.txt
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ chmod o=r MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ ls -l | grep MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
-rwx---r--  1 vibol vibol      1410 Nov 19 08:58 MS_LIG Nov 2025.txt
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ chmod o-r MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ ls -l | grep MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
-rwx------  1 vibol vibol      1410 Nov 19 08:58 MS_LIG Nov 2025.txt
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ chmod o+rwx MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ ls -l | grep MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
-rwx---rwx  1 vibol vibol      1410 Nov 19 08:58 MS_LIG Nov 2025.txt
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ chmod a+rwx MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
vibol@khcam:~/Desktop$ ls -l | grep MS_LIG\ Nov\ 2025.txt 
-rwxrwxrwx  1 vibol vibol      1410 Nov 19 08:58 MS_LIG Nov 2025.txt
